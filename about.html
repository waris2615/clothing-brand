Our Story

Home / About

Crafting Excellence
Since 1952

In the heart of Milan, a vision was born. ELEGANTE emerged from a small atelier where master craftsmen dedicated their lives to the art of tailoring. Our founder, Antonio Romano, believed that true luxury lies not in ostentation, but in the invisible details that transform ordinary garments into timeless treasures.

For over seven decades, we have upheld this philosophy. Each piece that bears our name represents hundreds of hours of meticulous handwork, the finest materials sourced from the world's most prestigious mills, and an unwavering commitment to perfection.

Today, ELEGANTE stands as a testament to the enduring power of craftsmanship. Our collections grace the wardrobes of discerning individuals across the globe, from the streets of Milan to the boulevards of Paris.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Exceptional Quality

We source only the finest materials—Italian woolens, French silks, and cashmere from Mongolia—ensuring each piece meets our exacting standards.

Handcrafted Care

Every stitch, every seam, every buttonhole is crafted by hand by our master artisans, preserving techniques passed down through generations.

Sustainable Future

We are committed to responsible luxury—ethical sourcing, sustainable practices, and creating pieces designed to last generations, not seasons.

Our Journey

Key milestones in our heritage

1952

The Beginning

Antonio Romano opens his first atelier in Milan's Via Montenapoleone, specializing in bespoke tailoring for discerning clientele.

1978

Parisian Expansion

ELEGANTE opens its first international boutique on Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, bringing Italian craftsmanship to France.

1995

Women's Collection

Launch of our inaugural women's ready-to-wear collection, blending feminine elegance with our signature artisanal techniques.

2026

The Future

Continuing our legacy while embracing innovation—sustainable materials, modern silhouettes, and timeless elegance.
